\title{\textbf{Average of per-cluster $\Delta\Sigma$ profiles vs.\
galaxy-level stacked $\Delta\Sigma$:\\
the WaZP small-$R$ failure does not reproduce in a
WaZP-matched simulation}}
\author{destacking project}
\date{2026 June 12}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The §9 diagnostic cell of \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb| forms the
unweighted mean of per-cluster $\DS$ profiles (from
\verb|xlensing.data.single_cluster|) and compares it to the stacked
$\DS$ from \verb|xlensing.data.stacked_signal| measured on the same
clusters but through the (z, NGALS, $\Delta m$) binning of
\verb|wazp_mass_richness_by_delta_m.ipynb|.  In the innermost radial
bins the comparison residual
$(\langle\DS\rangle_{\rm ind} - \DS_{\rm stack})/\DS_{\rm stack}$
drops sharply toward $-100\%$.  We reproduce the entire diagnostic on
a tightly matched simulation -- 15\,000 truth clusters drawn from a
realistic mass function (Tinker-08, restricted to the WaZP-relevant
$\log_{10} M \in [14.0, 15.0]$), Zheng-07 HOD, power-law satellite LF
$\Rightarrow$ per-cluster magnitude gap, McClintock+18 inversion to
$\Lambda$, the WaZP selection ($\Lambda > 25$, $\Delta m > 0$,
$0.10 \le z < 0.60$), source catalogues at galaxy density matched to
the WaZP-filtered metacal density ($\rho = 7.5\,
\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$), full DES Y3 shape noise
$\sigma_{e} = 0.27$, identical 5\,Mpc cone, identical radial bins, and
identical \verb|single_cluster| + \verb|stacked_signal| reductions on
the 24 $(z, \Lambda, \Delta m)$ stacks.  The simulation \emph{does
not} reproduce the dip-to-$-100\%$; per-stack residuals scatter around
zero within $\sim\pm 30\%$.  The proximate driver in the WaZP data is
a heavy-tailed per-cluster $\widehat\DS_c$ distribution: $3.4\%$ of
clusters report $|\widehat\DS_c(R_{\rm inner})| > 10^{4}\,
\Msun/\pc^{2}$, with a maximum of order $10^{7}$, none of which appear
in the simulation.  The unweighted mean of Eq.~\eqref{eq:avg} has no
defence against such outliers, and a handful per stack are enough to
swing the diagnostic across the entire plotted range.
\end{abstract}

\section{What we are testing}
\label{sec:test}

For a set of clusters $\{c\}$ and radial bins $\{b\}$ define
\begin{itemize}
\item the \emph{stacked} $\DS$ profile,
      $\DS_{\rm stack}(R_b)$, returned by
      \verb|xlensing.data.stacked_signal|;
\item the \emph{per-cluster} $\DS$ profile,
      $\widehat\DS_{c}(R_b)$, returned by
      \verb|xlensing.data.single_cluster| once per cluster $c$; and
\item the \emph{unweighted mean of per-cluster profiles}
      \begin{equation}
      \langle\DS\rangle_{\rm ind}(R_b)
       \;=\;
       \frac{1}{|\mathrm{used}_b|}
       \sum_{c \in \mathrm{used}_b}
        \widehat\DS_{c}(R_b),
       \label{eq:avg}
       \end{equation}
       summed over the clusters whose per-cluster $\widehat\DS_{c}$ is
       finite in bin $b$.
\end{itemize}
There is no covariance, no inverse-variance weighting, and no fit
involved -- only a comparison of two numbers per bin.  The residual we
plot is
\begin{equation}
\rho(R_b) \;=\;
\frac{\langle\DS\rangle_{\rm ind}(R_b) - \DS_{\rm stack}(R_b)}
     {\DS_{\rm stack}(R_b)} \,\times\, 100\,\%.
\label{eq:residual}
\end{equation}


\section{Estimators}
\label{sec:estimators}

Both estimators use the same per-galaxy quantities -- $\sigcrit$,
$e_t$, $e_x$, $w$, $R$, $M$ -- defined and divided by $10^{12}$ inside
\verb|xlensing.data.metacal_cluster_lensing|.  For each radial bin $b$
let $\mathbb{1}_b(g)$ be the in-bin indicator.

\subsection{Stacked: \texttt{stacked\_signal}}

Sums numerator and denominator at the galaxy level across every
cluster, divides once, and multiplies by the joint boost factor:
\begin{equation}
\DS_{\rm stack}(R_b)
\;=\;
B_{\rm stack}(R_b)\,
\frac{
   \sum_c \sum_g \mathbb{1}_b(g)\, e_{t,g}\, w_g / \Sigma_{{\rm crit},g}
}{
   \sum_c \sum_g \mathbb{1}_b(g)\, (1 + M_g)\, w_g / \Sigma_{{\rm crit},g}^{\,2}
},
\quad
B_{\rm stack}(R_b) = \frac{N_b^{\rm obs}}{N_b^{\rm random}}.
\label{eq:stacked}
\end{equation}
Every source contributes once with its natural inverse-variance weight
$w_g / \Sigma_{{\rm crit},g}^{\,2}$.

\subsection{Per-cluster: \texttt{single\_cluster}}

Performs a galaxy-level bootstrap inside one cluster's cone and returns
the bootstrap mean of the boost-corrected $\DS$:
\begin{equation}
\widehat\DS_{c}(R_b)
\;=\;
\underset{i \in \mathrm{valid}}{\Ex}
\left[\,
  B_{c,b}^{(i)} \cdot
  \frac{
    \sum_g \mathbb{1}_b(g)\, e_{t,g_i}\, w_{g_i} / \Sigma_{{\rm crit},g_i}
  }{
    \sum_g \mathbb{1}_b(g)\, (1 + M_{g_i})\, w_{g_i} / \Sigma_{{\rm crit},g_i}^{\,2}
  }
\,\right],
\label{eq:single}
\end{equation}
where $\{g_i\}$ is the $i$-th bootstrap resample of the cone's source
indices, $B_{c,b}^{(i)} = N_{c,b}^{(i)} / \mathrm{RR}_{c,b}$ is its
per-bin boost factor, and ``valid'' is the subset of iterations for
which every bin contains at least one resampled source.

The two estimators agree exactly in the limit of large per-bin source
counts but split in the small-count regime, as shown next.


\section{Simulated test: full WaZP-mirror catalogue}
\label{sec:sim}

The simulation uses \verb|destacking.simulation.generate_truth_catalog|
and \verb|make_test_data| to produce a cluster catalogue with the same
structure as the WaZP catalogue used by
\verb|wazp_mass_richness_by_delta_m.ipynb|.  Specifically:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Truth.}  Cluster redshifts uniform on $[0.10, 0.60)$,
      halo masses from the Tinker-08 mass function restricted to
      $\log_{10}(M_{\rm halo}/\Msun) \in [14.0, 15.0]$ (efficient
      drawing into the $\Lambda$-cut survivor distribution),
      \texttt{Zheng-07} HOD to assign satellite counts,
      power-law luminosity function for satellite magnitudes,
      Dutton--Maccio concentration with 0.1\,dex lognormal scatter.
\item \emph{Magnitude gap.}  Computed per cluster from the brightest
      satellite drawn from the satellite LF.
\item \emph{Observable $\Lambda$.}  Inverted from the true mass via
      McClintock+18 with Poisson scatter
      (\verb|make_test_data(..., poisson_richness=True)|).
\item \emph{Selection.}  $\Lambda > 25$, $\Delta m > 0$,
      $0.10 \le z < 0.60$ -- the same selection
      \verb|wazp_mass_richness_by_delta_m.ipynb| applies to
      \verb|wazp_real_data.fits|.  We oversample truth in batches
      until 15\,000 clusters survive the selection.
\item \emph{Per-cluster shear.}  Each cluster gets an independent
      metacal source catalogue from
      \verb|xlensing.sims.make_simple_random_cat| at galaxy density
      $\rho = 7.5\,\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$ (matched to the
      WaZP-filtered source density) with shape noise $\sigma_e = 0.27$
      and $z_{\rm src} \in (0, 2.5)$.  The analytic NFW shear is
      injected with
      \verb|xlensing.sims.apply_NFW_shear_region| at the cluster's
      true $(z, M, c)$.  The source catalogue is reduced with
      \verb|metacal_cluster_lensing| inside a 5\,Mpc cone and the
      per-cluster $\widehat\DS_c(R_b)$ comes from
      \verb|xlensing.data.single_cluster| with
      $N_{\rm boot} = 50$, on the same 8-bin radii grid
      $\mathtt{np.logspace}(-0.8, 0.6, 8)$ as
      \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb|.
\item \emph{Stack assignment.}  Identical to
      \verb|wazp_mass_richness_by_delta_m|: 3 redshift bins
      $[0.10, 0.30, 0.50, 0.60]$, 4 richness bins
      $[25, 50, 75, 100, \infty)$, and a per-cell-median $\Delta m$ split
      into low/high halves $\Rightarrow$ 24 stacks.
\item \emph{Per-stack diagnostic.}  Identical to §9 of
      \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb|: per stack, take the
      unweighted mean of per-cluster $\widehat\DS_c$ in the stack and
      compare to the stacked $\DS$ from \verb|stacked_signal| run on
      the same cluster's \verb|bkg| list.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Per-cluster source counts.}
At $\rho = 7.5\,\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$ in a 5\,Mpc cone at
$z = 0.4$, the cone area is $\sim 765\,\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$ and the
unfiltered yield is $\sim 5\,700$ sources per cluster.  After the
source-redshift cut $z_{\rm src} > 1.1\,z_{\rm lens} + 0.2$ enforced
internally by \verb|metacal_cluster_lensing|, the surviving yield is
$\sim 1\,300$ -- close to the $\sim 1\,500$ surviving WaZP sources per
cone.  The per-bin innermost yield is
$\langle N_{\rm inner}\rangle \approx 1\!-\!3$, matching the WaZP
regime.

\paragraph{Result.}
Figure~\ref{fig:sim-vs-real}, left panel.  Per-stack residuals scatter
around $\rho \approx 0$ within $\sim\pm 30\%$ at small $R$; no
systematic dip to $-100\%$ in any of the 18 populated stacks.  Across
the 15\,000 simulated clusters, the per-cluster $\widehat\DS_c$
distribution at the innermost bin has median
$\approx 150\,\Msun/\pc^{2}$, standard deviation $\approx 800$, and no
values outside $\pm 5\,000$ -- a heavy-tailed but bounded distribution
set by the per-galaxy shape noise of the few sources in the inner bin.


\section{The same test on the WaZP data}
\label{sec:data}

Inputs: \verb|notebooks/data/wazp_lensing_signals.pkl| (per-cluster
$\DS$ from \verb|single_cluster| on the WaZP $\Delta m > 0$ sample) and
\verb|notebooks/data/wazp_mr_dm_stacks.pkl| (stacked $\DS$ from
\verb|stacked_signal| on the same sample, binned per
\verb|wazp_mass_richness_by_delta_m.ipynb|).  For each $(z, \lambda,
\Delta m)$ stack we form the unweighted mean of the per-cluster
profiles whose IDs belong to that stack and compare to the stacked
profile interpolated in $\log_{10} R$ onto this notebook's 8-bin radii
grid.

\paragraph{Per-cluster source counts.}
The WaZP cone is 5\,Mpc; the pre-filtered DES Y3 source density after
the user's metacal redshift cut is $\sim 7\!-\!10$ gal/arcmin$^{2}$;
the surviving source count per cone at $z \sim 0.4$ after the
$z_s > 1.1\,z_l + 0.2$ cut is $\sim 1\,500$.  This puts the per-cluster
innermost-bin yield at $\langle N_{\rm inner}\rangle \in [1, 3]$.

\paragraph{Result.}
Figure~\ref{fig:sim-vs-real}, right panel.  Per-stack residuals
$\rho(R)$ are chaotic at small $R$, with several stacks plunging below
$-50\%$ and some past $-100\%$, while the outer-$R$ behaviour is closer
to zero.  The driver is exposed in Figure~\ref{fig:outliers}: across
the 11\,360 WaZP clusters with finite inner-bin signal, the per-cluster
$\widehat\DS_c$ distribution at $R = 0.158\,\Mpc$ has standard
deviation $\approx 1.7\!\times\!10^{5}$ and a maximum
$|\widehat\DS_c|$ of $\sim 1.4\!\times\!10^{7}$; $3.4\%$ of clusters
report $|\widehat\DS_c| > 10^{4}$.  None of these extreme values
appear in the simulation, whose maximum $|\widehat\DS_c|$ is
$\approx 4\,900$.  A handful of these outliers per stack is enough to
shift the unweighted mean by many standard deviations of the per-stack
sample.

\section{What is held the same and what differs}
\label{sec:diff}

The simulation is deliberately matched to the WaZP regime so the only
remaining differences between the two diagnostics are properties of
the real data, not of the test:

\begin{description}
\item[Matched.]
\begin{itemize}
\item Selection: $\Lambda > 25$, $\Delta m > 0$, $0.10 \le z < 0.60$.
\item Mass function: \texttt{generate\_truth\_catalog} uses Tinker-08;
      \texttt{wazp\_real\_data.fits} is the WaZP-detected catalogue,
      which is selection-effective rather than mass-function-pure but
      lives in the same mass range after the $\Lambda > 25$ cut.
\item Concentration prescription: Dutton--Maccio with 0.1\,dex
      lognormal scatter (sim) vs.\ whatever the real WaZP clusters
      have (data); both samples cover the same $M$, $z$ range.
\item Radial bins: $\mathtt{np.logspace}(-0.8, 0.6, 8)$ for the
      per-cluster reduction; 18-bin grid from
      \texttt{wazp\_mass\_richness\_by\_delta\_m} for the stack
      (interpolated onto the 8-bin grid for the diagnostic).
\item Source density: $\rho = 7.5\,\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$
      in both -- chosen to match the WaZP-filtered metacal density.
\item Source-redshift cut: $z_{\rm src} > 1.1\,z_{\rm lens} + 0.2$
      applied internally by \texttt{metacal\_cluster\_lensing}.
\item Shape noise: $\sigma_e = 0.27$ per component.
\item Per-cluster and stacked reductions: identical
      \texttt{single\_cluster} ($N_{\rm boot}=50$) and
      \texttt{stacked\_signal} calls on both sides.
\item Stack assignment: 3 z × 4 NGALS × 2 per-cell-median $\Delta m$ = 24
      stacks.
\end{itemize}
\item[Different.]
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Cluster-member contamination.}  The simulation injects a
      pure shear field on a uniform source catalogue; the true boost is
      $B \equiv 1$.  The real WaZP cones include cluster members in
      the inner bins, so the real $B(R)$ rises above unity at small
      $R$ and contributes its own scatter to the per-cluster
      bootstrap.
\item \emph{Source catalogue support.}  The sim uses a fresh,
      independent metacal-like draw per cluster (Poisson-density
      uniform in the cone), while the WaZP analysis cuts a cone out of
      the single DES Y3 metacal catalogue with its real spatial
      structure (large-scale structure, masking, edge effects).
\end{itemize}
\end{description}

If the simulation \emph{reproduces} the §9 dip-to-$-100\%$ behaviour
at small $R$, the cause is the per-cluster source-count regime, not
real-data-specific contamination or LSS effects.  If the simulation
does \emph{not} reproduce it, the failure is rooted in those
real-data-specific effects.


\section{Why \texttt{gamma\_scan} never surfaced this}
\label{sec:gammascan}

\verb|scripts/gamma_scan.py| reduces each simulated cluster with
\verb|xlensing.data.signal| (the plain numerator-over-denominator
routine, with no boost and no bootstrap) and feeds the per-cluster
$\widehat\DS_c$ vectors directly into a \emph{joint} likelihood that
fits the $\gamma$-of-interest across the population.  The cross-cluster
object $\langle\DS\rangle_{\rm ind}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:avg} -- which
\verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb|'s §9 cell forms as a diagnostic --
simply does not exist in the \verb|gamma_scan| pipeline.  The
comparison test is new in \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb|;
\verb|gamma_scan.py| could not have surfaced its failure mode.

The current report's Section~\ref{sec:sim} simulation is itself
already a stronger test than \verb|gamma_scan| would have been on this
front, because it uses the WaZP-matched source density rather than
\verb|gamma_scan|'s denser $\rho = 30\,\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$.
Even at the WaZP density the per-stack residuals are flat -- so the
failure visible in the real data is not a property of the
source-density regime alone.


\section{Summary}

The §9 diagnostic of \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb| collapses to
$\rho(R) \to -100\%$ at small $R$ on the WaZP catalogue.  Running
exactly the same diagnostic on a fully-matched simulation -- 15\,000
truth clusters from a realistic mass function, HOD, magnitude-gap
generator, the WaZP selection ($\Lambda > 25$, $\Delta m > 0$,
$0.10 \le z < 0.60$), the WaZP-filtered source density
($\rho = 7.5\,\mathrm{gal}/\mathrm{arcmin}^{2}$), the same shape
noise, the same radial bins, the same stack definitions, and the same
\verb|single_cluster| + \verb|stacked_signal| reductions -- recovers
$\rho \approx 0$ within statistical noise.  The failure is therefore
\emph{not} a property of the estimator pair at the WaZP source
density.

The proximate cause is exposed by Figure~\ref{fig:outliers}: the WaZP
per-cluster $\widehat\DS_c$ values at the innermost radial bin have a
heavy-tailed distribution with $3.4\%$ of clusters reporting
$|\widehat\DS_c| > 10^{4}\,\Msun/\pc^{2}$ and a maximum of order
$10^{7}\,\Msun/\pc^{2}$.  The unweighted mean of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:avg} has no defence against such outliers; a handful of
extreme contributors can swing the per-stack average across the entire
plotted range.  None of these outliers are present in the simulation,
which produces a tight, shape-noise-bounded
$\widehat\DS_c$ distribution capped at
$|\widehat\DS_c| \lesssim 5\,000$.

Since the simulation and the data share the per-cluster source count,
the radial layout, the selection, the shape-noise level, and the
identical \verb|single_cluster| / \verb|stacked_signal| codepaths,
the only ingredients that differ between the two diagnostics are:
\begin{itemize}
\item the presence of cluster-member contamination in real cones
      (an inner-bin galaxy over-density that drives the true
      $B(R) > 1$ but is absent in the uniform-source sim);
\item the spatial support of the DES Y3 metacal catalogue
      (large-scale structure, masking, edge effects);
\item details of the WaZP cluster catalogue (e.g.\ photometric
      misidentification at low signal-to-noise that does not occur in
      the truth catalogue).
\end{itemize}
One of those mechanisms is producing the extreme per-cluster
$\widehat\DS_c$ outliers in the real data.  Identifying which one is
the next step.

\subsection{Path forward}

\begin{itemize}
\item Audit the WaZP clusters with $|\widehat\DS_c(R_{\rm inner})|
      > 10^{4}\,\Msun/\pc^{2}$ for shared catalogue properties
      ($\Lambda$, $z_{\rm phot}$, BCG offset, edge-of-footprint, etc.)
      to localise the cause.
\item Inspect the per-cluster boost factor on those outliers: a
      degenerate $\mathrm{RR}_b$ (random catalogue empty in the
      innermost bin for a low-source-count cone) clamped to 1 inside
      \verb|single_cluster| would amplify the boost from
      $N_b / \mathrm{RR}_b$ to $N_b$ unboundedly.  Test by
      either (i) lifting the floor (return NaN in that bin), or (ii)
      bumping the random density so $\mathrm{RR}_b$ is well-sampled.
\item Replace the §9 unweighted mean by a median or trimmed mean.
      The median is already a much better summary at the inner bins
      (median $\approx 120\,\Msun/\pc^{2}$ for the real WaZP, close to
      the stacked value), and is unaffected by the outlier tail.
      This is a one-line change in
      \verb|wazp_lensing_signals.ipynb| §9.
\end{itemize}
