\section{Summary of today's work}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The day was spent in three intertwined directions:

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Code reorganisation.}  Runnable scripts were consolidated into
        a new \texttt{scripts/} folder; the DES~Y3 redshift-distribution
        helpers were promoted from inline notebook code to functions in
        \texttt{destacking.simulation}; the triaxiality machinery
        (\texttt{sample\_triaxial\_shapes}) and a Poisson-density flag in
        \texttt{make\_simple\_random\_cat} were added to
        \texttt{xlensing.sims}; the lensing worker was rewritten so every
        cluster draws its own random background catalogue.  All seeds in
        every script and in the notebook are now derived from a single
        \texttt{MASTER\_SEED} switch (default \texttt{None}, set to an
        integer for a fully reproducible run).
  \item \textbf{Bias fix.}  After tracing the
        $\hat\gamma$ low bias to a misaligned $c_0$ prior, \emph{Fix~A} from
        the previous bias report was applied to both the notebook and
        \texttt{scripts/gamma\_scan.py}: $c_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(4.57, 0.05)$,
        centred on $c_{\rm DM14}(M=10^{14}\,M_\odot, z=z_{\rm ref}=0.35) = 4.571$.
  \item \textbf{Two large simulation runs.}  A 19-point $\gamma$ scan with
        \texttt{scripts/gamma\_scan.py} and a 20-realisation
        error-bar-calibration run with \texttt{scripts/gamma\_repeat.py}
        produced the figures discussed below.
\end{enumerate}

Both scripts now also report a per-iteration wall time and a running ETA so
long scans can be left unattended.

%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Pipeline used for both runs}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Both runs share the same simulation/analysis configuration:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Truth catalogue: DES~Y3-like cluster $n(z) \propto z^2$ on
        $[0.2, 0.65]$, Tinker08 HMF in $\log_{10}M \in [13.5, 15]$,
        HOD-driven magnitude gap with $\Delta m_{\rm ref} = 0.1$\,mag.
  \item Triaxial shapes: \texttt{xlensing.sims.sample\_triaxial\_shapes} with
        CDM-like axis ratios and isotropic LOS.
  \item Miscentering simulation: $\fcen = 0.64$, Rayleigh scale
        $\sigmaoff = 0.32\,h^{-1}\,\mathrm{Mpc}$.
  \item Background: DES~Y3-like Smail--Schechter source $n(z)$
        ($\alpha=2$, $\beta=1.5$, $z_0=0.4$), source density
        $\sim 30\,\mathrm{gal\,arcmin}^{-2}$, fresh per-cluster catalogue with
        Poisson density.
  \item Inference: spherical-NFW forward model with fixed-parameter
        uniform population miscentering ($\mathrm{FMISS} = 1-\fcen$,
        $\sigmaoff$ as in the simulation) and the radial cut
        $R \geq \Rmin = 0.5$\,Mpc.
  \item Priors (after Fix~A):
        $c_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(4.57, 0.05)$,
        $\alpha \sim \mathcal{N}(-0.1, 0.05)$,
        $\beta \sim \mathcal{N}(-0.4, 0.2)$,
        $\gamma \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$,
        $\log M_{\rm norm} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 0.1)$.
  \item Sampler: \textsc{nautilus} with $n_{\rm live}=400$, parallelised
        over 15 cores.
\end{itemize}

\section{Run 1: $\gamma$ scan from $-0.5$ to $+0.5$}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\texttt{scripts/gamma\_scan.py} was run with 20 evenly spaced
$\gamma_{\rm true}$ values from $-0.5$ to $+0.5$ at $N_{\rm cluster}=5000$
per gamma.  Nineteen runs completed (the $\gamma_{\rm true}=+0.395$ run was
skipped); the cached per-gamma JSON summaries live in
\texttt{scripts/gamma\_scan\_outputs/run\_2026-05-19T16-12-56/}.

\paragraph{Statistics across the scan.}  Across the 19 successful runs we
find
\begin{equation}
  \langle\hat\gamma - \gamma_{\rm true}\rangle = +0.144,\quad
  \mathrm{std}(\hat\gamma - \gamma_{\rm true}) = 0.384,\quad
  \langle\sigma_\gamma^{\rm post}\rangle = 0.151.
  \label{eq:scan_stats}
\end{equation}
The mean residual is small (the constant low bias diagnosed before Fix~A is
gone), but the run-to-run scatter is more than $2\times$ the average
posterior $\sigma$.  Several runs report $4$–$11\,\sigma$ apparent biases
even though the inference is internally well-behaved.  These outliers are
not isolated bugs: they are a direct symptom of the error-bar calibration
issue confirmed by Run~2.

\section{Run 2: 20 realisations at $\gamma_{\rm true} = 0.2$}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\texttt{scripts/gamma\_repeat.py} was run with \texttt{TRUE\_GAMMA = 0.2},
\texttt{MASTER\_SEED = None} (fresh OS entropy at every RNG), and 20
realisations of the full pipeline.  Outputs live in
\texttt{scripts/gamma\_repeat\_outputs/run\_2026-05-19T18-15-45/}.  Each
realisation produced its own corner, $M$–$c$ recovery, $\gamma$ marginal,
truth-distribution, triaxiality-diagnostic, etc.\ plots; the two aggregate
plots are reproduced below.

\begin{figure}[h]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{scripts/gamma_repeat_outputs/run_2026-05-19T18-15-45/gamma_residuals.png}
  \caption{Per-realisation $\hat\gamma_k - \gamma_{\rm true}$ for the 20
  realisations.  Error bars are the 68\% posterior credible intervals;
  the dashed black line is $\hat\gamma = \gamma_{\rm true}$.  The
  \textsf{tomato} band is the empirical $\sigma_{\rm runs}$ of the
  medians.}
  \label{fig:repeat_residuals}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[h]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.95\textwidth]{scripts/gamma_repeat_outputs/run_2026-05-19T18-15-45/posteriors_overlay.png}
  \caption{KDE overlay of all 20 marginal $\gamma$ posteriors (viridis,
  one curve per realisation) against the truth $\gamma_{\rm true} = 0.2$
  (\textsf{crimson} dashed) and the $\mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ prior
  (\textsf{darkorange} dashed).}
  \label{fig:repeat_overlay}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Calibration result.}  The 20 realisations give
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{mean}(\hat\gamma) = 0.196,\qquad
  \mathrm{std}(\hat\gamma_k) = \sigma_{\rm runs} = 0.399,\qquad
  \langle\sigma_\gamma^{\rm post}\rangle = 0.159.
\end{equation}
The recovery is \emph{unbiased on average} ($\mathrm{mean}(\hat\gamma -
\gamma_{\rm true}) = -0.004$, well inside any individual posterior $\sigma$),
which confirms that Fix~A successfully removed the low bias from the
misaligned $c_0$ prior.

The calibration ratio, however, is
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\frac{\sigma_{\rm runs}}{\langle\sigma_\gamma^{\rm post}\rangle}
        = \frac{0.399}{0.159} = 2.51\;}
  \label{eq:cal_ratio}
\end{equation}
where a perfectly calibrated posterior would give a ratio of $1$.  This is
the headline result of the day: the per-realisation posterior credible
intervals \textbf{under-estimate the true scatter by a factor of about
2.5}.

\paragraph{Why this matches Run~1.}  The factor-2.5 mismatch in Run~2 is
the same as the apparent ``many-$\sigma$ biases'' seen in Run~1.  Each
gamma in the scan is a single realisation, so it has a scatter of
$\sim 0.4$ around the truth (Run~2 calibration); but its quoted posterior
$\sigma$ is $\sim 0.15$, giving the deceptive impression of a $\sim 2.7\sigma$
bias for typical realisations.  Outliers ($4$–$11\sigma$) are just the
expected tails of this under-coverage.

%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Interpretation: what the factor-2.5 ratio tells us}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A run-to-run scatter that exceeds the per-run posterior $\sigma$ by a factor
$\sim 2.5$ means the joint Gaussian likelihood used by the analysis is
\emph{too narrow} given the variance in the data-generating process.  The
likely culprits, in approximate order of expected magnitude:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textit{Triaxiality scatter in the inner profile.}  The radial cut
        $R \geq 0.5$\,Mpc was tuned to suppress the \emph{bias} from the
        spherical/triaxial mismatch, but the per-cluster amplitude
        \emph{scatter} from random axis ratios and LOS orientations is not
        modelled in the covariance.  This scatter is significant (10--30\%
        of $\dsig$ at intermediate $R$) and propagates directly into a
        wider $\gamma$ posterior than the formal shape-noise-only
        likelihood predicts.
  \item \textit{Per-cluster miscentering realisation.}  The forward model
        uses the population-level Rayleigh miscentering correction; the
        truth has a specific offset per cluster.  The leftover per-cluster
        mismatch acts as additional scatter not absorbed by the
        shape-noise covariance.
  \item \textit{Background-catalogue variance.}  The per-cluster background
        catalogue is now an independent draw with Poisson density; the
        shape-noise diagonal covariance assumes a smooth $\bar n$ and so
        underestimates the per-cluster variance.
\end{itemize}

%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Concrete next steps suggested by the figures}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
  \item Re-introduce a controlled covariance-inflation parameter
        $f_{\rm intrinsic}$ (this was tried as a global free parameter and
        rolled back earlier in the project, but at the time the bias from
        the misaligned $c_0$ prior dominated and obscured the calibration
        improvement; now that Fix~A removes that bias, $f_{\rm intrinsic}$
        should be retried as a \emph{fixed} value calibrated against
        \texttt{gamma\_repeat.py}'s ratio of 2.5).
  \item Use the per-cluster triaxiality residuals computed by the existing
        \texttt{triaxiality\_diagnostic.png} plot to fit a parametric
        $\sigma_{\rm tri}(R)$ model and add it on the diagonal of every
        $\mathbf{C}_i$.
  \item Bin a subset of clusters by $\Delta m$ and check whether the run-to-run
        scatter scales as $1/\sqrt{N}$; this would discriminate between
        per-cluster scatter (no improvement with $N$) and shot noise
        (improvement with $N$).
  \item Repeat \texttt{gamma\_repeat.py} at $N=10\,000$ and $N=20\,000$ to
        confirm that the $\sigma_{\rm runs}/\langle\sigma^{\rm post}\rangle$
        ratio stays near $2.5$ rather than drifting toward unity --- a sign
        that the missing variance is structural, not statistical.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Conclusions}
%──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Fix~A removes the misaligned-$c_0$-prior bias: the mean of the recovered
$\hat\gamma$ across 20 independent realisations of the same noise realisation
matches the truth $\gamma_{\rm true} = 0.2$ to better than $0.005$.  The
posterior credible intervals are however \emph{too narrow} by a factor of
$\sim 2.5$, which is the single biggest remaining issue in the inference and
the natural target of the next round of work.
